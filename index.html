Who We Are

About AfricaYouth In
Green Hydrogen (AYIGH)

AYIGH is a youth-driven platform empowering young Africans in green hydrogen, clean energy, climate innovation, and sustainable development through learning, networking, collaboration, and leadership.

We are building Africa's largest youth movement for green hydrogen, connecting students, young professionals, climate innovators, and energy enthusiasts across the continent.

Our Vision

A Green Hydrogen Future
Led by African Youth

To position African youth at the forefront of the global green hydrogen economy, driving sustainable development, innovation, and climate leadership.

Our Mission

Building a connected generation of African youth equipped to lead the green hydrogen and clean molecule economy. We bridge education, industry, policy, and grassroots action to create a just and inclusive energy transition.

Why We Exist

Africa has the world's greatest renewable energy potential, yet young people are often excluded from the green economy. AYIGH exists to ensure youth are not just beneficiaries but leaders of the hydrogen revolution.

What Drives Us

We believe that empowering youth with knowledge, networks, and opportunities in green hydrogen will unlock sustainable prosperity for Africa while tackling the climate crisis.

Our Strategic Goals

How We Create Impact

Youth Empowerment

Mentorship, leadership bootcamps, and hydrogen scholarships across the continent.

Clean Energy Innovation

Fueling startups, green hydrogen pilots, and R&D by young African innovators.

Policy & Leadership

Amplify youth voices in energy policy discussions, ensuring decisions reflect inclusivity, innovation, and just transition principles.

Continental Collaboration

Build strong networks across Africa to connect youth organizations, researchers, and entrepreneurs for continental knowledge-sharing and joint projects.

Innovation & Skills Development

Provide training, mentorship, and incubation platforms to equip youth with technical and entrepreneurial skills in the green hydrogen value chain.

Africa's Green Future

Positioning Africa as a global powerhouse for sustainable hydrogen solutions.
