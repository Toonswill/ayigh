About YIGH

Youth In Green Hydrogen

YIGH is a youth-led platform focused on building Africa's next generation of leaders in green hydrogen, clean energy, climate innovation, and sustainable development.

YIGH exists to educate, connect, inspire, and empower young people to actively participate in the future clean molecule economy through knowledge sharing, collaboration, innovation, and leadership development.

Our Vision

Bridging the Gap

YIGH aims to bridge the gap between African youth and the rapidly growing global green hydrogen economy by preparing young people with the awareness, skills, networks, and mindset needed to participate in the future of sustainable energy.
