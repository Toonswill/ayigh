About YIGH

YIGH is a youth-driven platform empowering young Africans in green hydrogen, clean energy, climate innovation, and sustainable development through learning, networking, collaboration, and leadership. We are the spark of Africa's hydrogen revolution.

Our Vision for Africa

Youth Empowerment

Mentorship, leadership bootcamps, and hydrogen scholarships across the continent.

Clean Energy Innovation

Our Mission

Building a connected generation of African youth equipped to lead the green hydrogen and clean molecule economy. We bridge education, industry, policy, and grassroots action.
