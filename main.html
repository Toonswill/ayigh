About YIGH

Youth In Green Hydrogen

YIGH is a pan-African youth-led platform empowering young Africans to lead in green hydrogen, clean energy, and climate innovation.

We educate, connect, inspire, and empower young people to actively shape the future clean molecule economy across the continent.
